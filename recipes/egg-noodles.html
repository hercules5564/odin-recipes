This is the recipe of how to make egg noodles

In this recpie we will learn how to make egg noodles in a healthy and a tasty way

Ingredients

  • flour
  • oil
  • masalas
  • eggs
  • Milk
  • sauces
  • vegies

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Stir flour and salt together in a large bowl. Add eggs, milk, and butter.
  3. Knead dough until smooth, about 5 minutes. Let rest in a covered bowl for 10 minutes.
  4. Cook noodles in a large pot of salted boiling water until al dente, about 5 minutes.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
